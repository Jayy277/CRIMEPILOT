COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge M. Durai
CASE: CP-CASE-101055

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that the complainant stated that when they returned home from vacation, they found the front lock broken and jewelry worth inr 671055 stolen.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Theft & Burglary and is sentenced accordingly.
