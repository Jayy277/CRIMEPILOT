COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Justice Anand Verma
CASE: CP-CASE-101456

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that a 10-year-old child was abducted outside their school by two individuals in a black suv. a ransom call was received later.

Decision:
The accused is acquitted due to lack of standard corroborative evidence.
