COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Rajesh K. Mehta
CASE: CP-CASE-100544

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that the victim was assaulted by a group of individuals near a local market, sustaining severe head injuries and a fractured arm.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Assault & Hurt and is sentenced accordingly.
