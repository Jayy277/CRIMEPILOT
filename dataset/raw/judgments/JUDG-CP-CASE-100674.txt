COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Justice Anand Verma
CASE: CP-CASE-100674

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that a dead body with multiple stab wounds was discovered in an abandoned building. police suspect a pre-planned murder.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Murder & Homicide and is sentenced accordingly.
