COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: District and Sessions Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Rajesh K. Mehta
CASE: CP-CASE-100377

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that the victim received a phishing call pretending to be a bank official, leading to an unauthorized debit of inr 61313 from their bank account.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Cyber Crime & Fraud and is sentenced accordingly.
