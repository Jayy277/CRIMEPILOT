COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: District and Sessions Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Shalini Joshi
CASE: CP-CASE-100374

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that the victim received a phishing call pretending to be a bank official, leading to an unauthorized debit of inr 1111677 from their bank account.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Cyber Crime & Fraud and is sentenced accordingly.
