COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Rajesh K. Mehta
CASE: CP-CASE-100541

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that the victim received a phishing call pretending to be a bank official, leading to an unauthorized debit of inr 681655 from their bank account.

Decision:
The accused is acquitted due to lack of standard corroborative evidence.
