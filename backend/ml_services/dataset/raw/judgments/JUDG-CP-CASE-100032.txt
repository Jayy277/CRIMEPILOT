COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Rajesh K. Mehta
CASE: CP-CASE-100032

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that accused voluntarily caused bodily harm to a passerby after a heated argument, using a wooden stick.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Assault & Hurt and is sentenced accordingly.
