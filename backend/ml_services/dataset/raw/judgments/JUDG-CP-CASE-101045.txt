COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge Shalini Joshi
CASE: CP-CASE-101045

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that accused lured a young girl under false pretenses of a job offer and held her captive in a remote village.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Kidnapping & Abduction and is sentenced accordingly.
