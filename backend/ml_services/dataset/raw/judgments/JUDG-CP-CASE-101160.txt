COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge M. Durai
CASE: CP-CASE-101160

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that a physical altercation broke out between neighbors over a parking space, resulting in the accused hitting the victim with an iron rod causing grievous hurt.

Decision:
The accused is acquitted due to lack of standard corroborative evidence.
