COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: Metropolitan Magistrate Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge M. Durai
CASE: CP-CASE-101907

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that accused created a fake social media profile using the complainant's photos and extorted money from their contacts.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Cyber Crime & Fraud and is sentenced accordingly.
