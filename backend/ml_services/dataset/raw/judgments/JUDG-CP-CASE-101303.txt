COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Judge M. Durai
CASE: CP-CASE-101303

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that a thief broke into a commercial shop at night by drilling a hole in the rear wall and stole cash amount of inr 687545.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Theft & Burglary and is sentenced accordingly.
