COURT JUDGMENT RECORD
IN THE COURT OF: High Court
PRESIDING JUDGE: Justice H.S. Sangwan
CASE: CP-CASE-101169

Facts:
On examination of witnesses and documents, it is observed that victim was abducted from a bus stop by masked men. the family received threats demanding property papers.

Decision:
The Court finds the accused guilty of Kidnapping & Abduction and is sentenced accordingly.
